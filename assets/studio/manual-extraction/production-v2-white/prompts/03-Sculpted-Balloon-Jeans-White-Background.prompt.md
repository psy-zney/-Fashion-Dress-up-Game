# Sculpted Balloon Jeans — White Background

Use case: identity-preserve

Use the new white-background model as the definitive identity, compact-hair,
accessory, pose and framing anchor. Put only the supplied high-waisted,
dark-indigo sculpted balloon jeans on her. Render very wide legs from heavy
cotton denim twill with a real waistband, loops, button, fly and pockets.
Curved panels must be physically joined by continuous tan double topstitching,
with subtle indigo grain, natural fold depth and dark occlusion at sewn joins.
Every curved line must read as a seam, not a print. Keep the feet bare and the
backdrop uniform white. No flyaways, checkerboard, halo, blur, text, watermark
or crop.
