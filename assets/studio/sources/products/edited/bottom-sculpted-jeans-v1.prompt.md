## Product image prompt

Transform the fashion drawing into a photorealistic, physically plausible pair of real denim jeans while following the design extremely closely. Keep the front-facing high waist, dark indigo color, very wide sculptural balloon legs, button and zipper fly, belt loops, curved riveted pockets, all curved panel seams, geometric lower-leg seams, subtle multicolor topstitch accents, and raw frayed hems. Use authentic heavyweight denim weave with restrained seam fading and natural structured folds. Present one complete pair centered in a straight-on catalog view. No person, mannequin, hanger, props, text, logo, watermark, or cropped edges.

## Model-worn prompt

Dress the immutable 1024 x 1536 model master in the generated jeans. Change only the lower-body garment and preserve the model's identity, face, hair, upper body, bandeau, arms, hands, pose, proportions, feet, camera, alignment, lighting, style, and canvas. Fit the high waistband at the existing waist, keep the distinctive panel construction and wide balloon silhouette, and extend the raw hems almost to the floor while leaving the feet available for footwear layering.
